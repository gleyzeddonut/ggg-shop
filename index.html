Blossom
BL-03 · MIDI chord instrument · AU / VST3 / App

One finger. Full chords.

Hold a chord key in your keyboard's bottom octave, play a note, and Blossom voices the chord and sends it straight to your synth or DAW.

See Blossom→
Lock In
LI-05 · Take alignment · Mac app

Tight takes, no editing.

Drop in a guide and its takes — vocal doubles, drum loops, instruments. Lock In locks every take to the guide's timing and hands the files back to your DAW. Formerly Vocal Lock.

See Lock In→
CR8
CR-08 · Sample digger · Mac app

Sounds you forgot you had.

CR8 learns your whole library's tempo, key and role, rolls loops that fit together, and drags them into your DAW. Lock what works, reroll the rest.

See CR8→
