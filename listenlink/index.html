Listen­Link

Stream your master bus to anyone.

An audio plugin for your DAW's master bus that streams whatever passes through it to a web link. Your listener opens the link in a browser and presses play — no account, no app, no setup on their end.

Download for macOS Source code VST3 · AU
Fig. 01 — The whole interface 48 kHz · 16-bit

How it works

3 steps · under a minute

01 Drop it on your master

Add ListenLink (VST3 or AU) to your master bus. Audio passes through untouched — it just gets copied to the stream.

02 Create a link

Share on your network for anyone on the same Wi-Fi, or create a public link that works anywhere. Copy, send.

03 They press play

The link opens a listener page in any browser, typically less than half a second behind you. It shows its own meters and a listener count.

A public link in one click

ListenLink spins up a secure Cloudflare tunnel — no account, no port forwarding. Send the link to a client across the world; they press play. The link lives as long as your session does.

Lossless when it matters, light when it doesn't

Switch between lossless PCM and Opus on the fly — listeners re-sync in under a second. The bandwidth readout tells you exactly what each listener costs your connection.

Install

~1 minute
  1. Download and unzip ListenLink-0.2.0-mac.zip.
  2. Copy the two bundles into your plugin folders (instructions are in the zip).
  3. Restart your DAW or rescan plugins, then add ListenLink to your master bus.

For public links, install the free tunnel client once: brew install cloudflared. macOS may ask to allow incoming network connections the first time — allow it. Local-network streaming works without any of this.
