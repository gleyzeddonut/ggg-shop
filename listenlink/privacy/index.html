Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026

ListenLink is a free audio plugin made by GGG (Dan Gleyzer). It streams the audio from your DAW to a web link so other people can listen live. This policy explains what the plugin, the listener page and this website do with information. The short version: ListenLink has no accounts, we run no server that stores your audio, and the only personal data involved is what you choose to connect.

Streaming audio

Audio leaves your computer through a Cloudflare tunnel and goes straight to the browsers of people who open your link. We do not receive, record or store any of it. Streams pass through Cloudflare’s network, which is subject to Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Your public link

To keep one link working across sessions, the plugin registers a random stream ID, an ownership token and the current tunnel address with our link service at gggaudio.store. That record contains no name, email or account information and is deleted seven days after the stream was last active, or as soon as you press Stop.

Google Docs session notes (optional)

You can connect a Google account so ListenLink can attach a Google Doc to your stream as session notes. If you do:

  • ListenLink asks Google for permission to create documents and see the documents it created (the drive.file scope), and for your email address so the plugin can show which account is connected. It cannot see the rest of your Google Drive.
  • Docs are created in your Google Drive and belong to you. ListenLink sets them to “anyone with the link can edit” so listeners can type in them, and shares the doc link with people who open your stream link.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your computer, in ~/Library/Application Support/ListenLink/. It is never sent to us. We operate no server that receives your Google data; the plugin talks to Google directly.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the plugin’s notes menu, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting does not delete docs you created.

ListenLink’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Listeners

The listener page sets no cookies, runs no analytics and asks for no account. A listener’s connection passes through Cloudflare on its way to the streamer’s computer. If a session-notes doc is attached, the page shows a link to it; opening that link takes the listener to Google Docs, under Google’s terms and privacy policy.

Updates and downloads

When you open the plugin it checks GitHub once for a newer release, and the first time you create a link it downloads Cloudflare’s tunnel tool from GitHub. GitHub sees those requests like any download.

This website

gggaudio.store uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies or cross-site tracking. Listener pages are not included.

Children

ListenLink is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Questions: info@gggaudio.store.
