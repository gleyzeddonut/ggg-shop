Lock In

Tight takes, no editing.

Drop in a guide and the takes that go with it. Lock In moves every double, harmony, drum loop and instrument onto the guide's timing, follows its pitch if you want, and hands back files ready to drop into your DAW. Formerly Vocal Lock.

Download — Free Mac app · macOS 12+ · Apple silicon & Intel
Fig. 01 — Guide, takes, settings one window

How it works

3 steps · processes as you go

01 Drop the guide

The lead vocal, the main drum loop, or whichever performance the others should follow. WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 or M4A.

02 Drop the takes

Doubles, harmonies, ad-libs, loops. Each one is processed as soon as it loads, and turning a knob updates it in about a second.

03 Drag them back

Drag lanes straight into your DAW or the Finder, or Export all. Aligned takes come out at the guide's length, so they line up with the lead.

Align, or align and follow pitch

Align moves each take onto the guide's timing and leaves its notes alone. Align + Pitch also bends it onto the guide's pitch. Set every take at once in the Global card, or click a take and pick its own mode. One tightness knob and one pitch knob for all of them, with a nudge up or down for any take that needs it.

Fig. 03 — Drums and instruments hits moved whole · stereo kept

Drums and instruments too

Drop in a drum loop and Lock In recognises it: each hit is paired with the guide's closest hit and moved onto it whole, so nothing is stretched and the hits sound as they did. For guitars, keys and anything that plays chords, pick Instrument: the take follows the guide's timing and its chords stay whole. Stereo takes stay stereo.

No double? Make one

Set a take to Double and Lock In sings it again, with the small timing and pitch drift of a real second pass. Mono or a stereo pair, the double alone or mixed with the lead. Press New take for a different performance.

Hear it before you commit

Play everything together, solo any take against the guide, or hear the guide alone, with mute, solo and pan on every lane. Flip Hear original to compare with the takes as they were recorded.

Right inside Ableton Live

with the Live script · full installer

01 Pick the guide

Click the lead in the Arrangement and press Ctrl‑Opt‑G. It becomes the guide, right where it sits in the song.

02 Swap a take in place

Click a double and press Ctrl‑Opt‑R. It's aligned and put back exactly where it was, with the original kept on a take lane. One Cmd‑Z undoes it. Want it tighter? Turn the knob and press it again.

03 Pick up where you left off

Save the guide, the takes and every setting as a session, and open it again later. Change both shortcuts in Settings.

And inside Logic Pro

no script · same two shortcuts

01 Pick the guide

Select the lead's region and press Ctrl‑Opt‑G. It becomes the guide, right where it sits in the song.

02 Swap a take in place

Select a double and press Ctrl‑Opt‑R. It's aligned and put back on the same track at the same position. Cmd‑Z twice brings the original back.

03 Or bring regions in

Select a region and click From DAW in the app to add it as the guide or a take, and leave the project as it is. macOS asks once to allow Lock In under Accessibility.

Spec sheet

LI-05 · v0.11.0
InstallOpen the disk image and drag Lock In to Applications.
Runs onmacOS 12+ · Apple silicon and Intel · Signed and notarized
Works withAny DAW. Drop files in from the Finder or your DAW, drag the results back. Ableton Live users can also pick clips straight from the Arrangement, and swap takes for their aligned versions in place, with the Live script, which comes with the full installer. Logic Pro users can do both with no script: select a region and press the shortcut.
SessionsSave and open .vlsession files. The guide, the takes and every setting; the audio stays where it is and is processed again on opening.
ModesAlign · Align + Pitch · Double · Drums · Instrument Global mode sets every aligned vocal take at once; each take can still have its own. Drum takes are recognised when you add them.
ReadsWAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, M4A. Any sample rate. Takes at a different rate from the guide are converted.
Writes32-bit float WAV. Aligned takes at the guide's sample rate and length; doubles at the take's. Drums and Instrument takes keep their stereo.
PrivacyNo account, no analytics. Your audio never leaves your Mac. It checks a version file for updates at most every six hours.
