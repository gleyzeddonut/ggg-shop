UAD Doctor

See the −38 coming.

A menu-bar dot for Universal Audio Apollo and UAD-2 rigs. It shows your DSP load, warns before the −38 error, and unfreezes your DAW with one click.

Download — Free Mac app · macOS 13+ · Apple silicon & Intel
Fig. 01 — Green, yellow, red menu bar · click to open

How it works

3 steps · no setup

01 Look up

A dot and a number in your menu bar: the same DSP figure the UAD Meter shows. No window, no Dock icon.

02 Watch the colour

Green: fine. Yellow: save, something is heading for trouble. Red: DSP over 90%, your DAW frozen, or the device gone.

03 Recover Now

One click restarts UA's engine, then CoreAudio only if needed. Your DAW comes back without a power-cycle. Auto-fix can do the first step for you.

Sees what the meter hides

The UAD Meter averages all chips; one can be missing deadlines while the total looks fine. UAD Doctor also watches the symptoms: skipped audio cycles, a frozen DAW, UA's error reports, clock and Thunderbolt drops.

Evidence, not guesswork

Every UA error and freeze is saved as an incident: the UA report with per-chip load, the macOS audio log, Thunderbolt state, top processes, your DAW's log. History Report lines them up.
