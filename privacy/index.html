Privacy

GGG (Dan Gleyzer) makes small audio software. We have no accounts, no analytics inside the apps, and no interest in your data. This page says exactly what does leave your Mac, and why. ListenLink has its own policy because it streams audio.

This website

gggaudio.store uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies or cross-site tracking. The site sets no cookies of its own.

Buying

Checkout is run by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record. Paddle collects your email address, payment details and the country you're in to process the payment and the tax on it, under Paddle's privacy policy. Paddle sends us your email address and the order number. We store those together with the license key we issued, so we can send the key again and handle refunds. We don't see your card or payment details. Blossom purchases go through Gumroad under Gumroad's policy.

We use your email only for your license: to send the key, to answer your messages, and in rare cases to tell you about a problem that affects your purchase. No newsletters, nothing shared or sold.

The CR8 app

  • Your library stays on your Mac. Analysis of your sounds runs locally. File names, audio, tags and the catalog it builds are never uploaded.
  • Activating a key sends the key and the app's version number to our license service, once. The service answers with a signed license that the app keeps locally. Nothing about your Mac, user name or hardware is sent.
  • Periodic checks. About every six hours while running, the app fetches the update manifest from GitHub and the list of revoked keys from our license service. Those are plain downloads with no identifiers beyond the app's version in the request.
  • Splice tags. If Splice is installed, the app reads its tag database from your own disk, for search. That data isn't sent anywhere.

UAD Doctor and Blossom

UAD Doctor makes one daily request for its version file, with no identifiers. Blossom makes no network requests.

Where it lives

Our license records are stored with Cloudflare (Workers KV) in their data centres. Paddle holds the order records. Email to info@gggaudio.store is handled by our mail provider like any email.

Your rights

Ask and we'll tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it; deleting a license record means the key stops working. Email info@gggaudio.store. For the payment records themselves, Paddle is the controller and has its own process.

Children

Our software isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Questions: info@gggaudio.store.
