How it works

3 steps · no setup

01 Dig

Add a folder or a whole drive. CR8 listens to every file in the background, on all your cores: tens of thousands of sounds are ready in minutes, and it keeps going while you work.

02 Roll

Pick a key and tempo, or take the ones it suggests. Each slot gets a sound that fits: drums, bass, tonal, vocal, texture. Reroll a slot as any role, or type what you want, like “synth” or “vinyl”.

03 Drag

Drag a loop, a beat, or the whole set into Ableton, Logic or FL Studio. Everything lands stretched and tuned to the session. Your original files are never touched.

Your whole library, sorted.

Dig shows what you own by role, how many sounds you've pulled back into a set after years untouched, and a few quick confirms: one-tap questions where the analyser isn't sure. Each answer makes the next reroll better.

Lock what works. Reroll the rest.

Every card is a slot with its own gain, pan, mute and solo. Lock a card and it stays through every reroll; press R and the others change around it. Set where a loop starts, fix a wrong tempo or key, or mark a sound as a one-shot and drop it into the step sequencer for a beat underneath.

Runs onmacOS 14+ on Apple silicon (M1 or later). Signed and notarized. Intel Macs aren't supported.
ReadsWAV, AIFF, CAF, FLAC, MP3, M4A and AAC, from any folder or drive, including external drives that come and go.

OutputLoops and beats rendered at the session tempo and key, dragged straight into a track or exported to ~/Music/CR8. Originals are never changed.
PrivacyNo account, no analytics. CR8 only goes online to check for updates, to activate your key once, and to fetch the list of refunded keys. Nothing from your library leaves your Mac.
