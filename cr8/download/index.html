CR8 plugin · v0.3.6 · Beta

CR8 in your DAW

An AU and VST3 instrument that plays your CR8 set in time with your DAW. It runs on the CR8 app, so install that first. Tested in Ableton Live 12.

  1. Open the installer and follow it. It puts CR8 in your Audio Unit and VST3 folders.
  2. In your DAW, add CR8 (by GGG) to an instrument or MIDI track.
  3. Press play in your DAW. Reroll, lock and drag, as in the app.
