BLOSSOM

Setup Guide

Hold a chord key in the violet bottom octave, play a note above it, and Blossom sends the full chord to your synth.

Two ways to run it

Pick your setup

The app connects MIDI ports directly. Use it to play hardware synths — no DAW needed. It can also feed a DAW over a virtual port.

The plugin (AU + VST3) runs inside Logic Pro or Ableton Live and follows the session tempo.

Both have the same features. One installer sets up both.

One installer

Install

  1. Open the downloaded Blossom.dmg.
  2. Double-click Install Blossom.pkg.
  3. Follow the installer. It places the app in Applications and the AU and VST3 plugins in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.
  4. Restart your DAW, or rescan plug-ins.

The app's first launch takes about a minute to set up. After that it opens instantly.

Standalone

The Blossom app

Play a hardware synth

  1. Set IN to your keyboard.
  2. Set OUT to your synth. Set CH to the synth's MIDI channel (usually 1).
  3. Play. Keys above the bottom octave light up on the piano and sound as chords.

Click ↻ Refresh after plugging in new gear.

Play a soft synth in your DAW

Route the chords through an IAC bus, macOS's built-in virtual MIDI port:

  1. Open Audio MIDI Setup → Window → Show MIDI Studio. Double-click IAC Driver.
  2. Tick Device is online. If the Ports list is empty, click + to add IAC Bus 1. Click Apply. Restart your DAW.
  3. In Blossom, set OUT to the IAC bus.
  4. In the DAW, set the synth track's MIDI input to the IAC bus — not “All Ins”, which also passes your raw notes and doubles everything. Arm the track or set Monitor to In.

Updates

The version number in the bottom-right checks for updates at launch. When one is available it shows get v2.2.0. Click it to install, then click relaunch ↻. Click the plain version number to check manually.

AU · VST3

The Blossom plugin

Logic Pro

On a software instrument track, click the MIDI FX slot (above the instrument) and choose Audio Units → Blossom → Blossom. Play the track. No routing needed.

Ableton Live

Live has no MIDI FX slot, so Blossom loads as an instrument and you route its MIDI to your synth with two tracks:

  1. Track 1: load Blossom. Set MIDI From to your keyboard. Set Monitor to In, or arm the track.
  2. Track 2: load your synth. Set MIDI From to the Blossom track. In the second dropdown below it, pick Blossom (the device) — not Pre FX.
  3. Set Track 2's Monitor to In, or arm it. Play.

When it doesn't

Troubleshooting

No sound from the app
Make sure your keyboard sends MIDI over USB (Prophet-6: GLOBALS → MIDI Out Select → USB). Then click Blossom's on-screen piano. Sound means the keyboard side is the problem; no sound means the synth side is.
Blossom isn't listed in Ableton
Look under VST3 in the browser. Make sure the VST3 folder is enabled in Settings → Plug-Ins. After an update, hold Option and click Rescan.
Blossom isn't in Logic's instrument list
It's in the MIDI FX slot above the instrument, not the instrument slot.
Chords double up or raw notes leak through
Something is listening to the keyboard directly. In Live: the second MIDI-From dropdown is on Pre FX. With the app + IAC bus: a DAW track is set to “All Ins”.
The arp ignores the beat
Plugin: the arp locks to the beat only while the transport is playing. App: it needs MIDI clock on the SYNC port — see the IAC steps above.
